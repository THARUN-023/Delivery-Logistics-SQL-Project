# 📊 Business Insights

The analysis of delivery, driver, vehicle, payment, and customer review data produced the following business insights.

## 🚚 Driver Performance Insights

- The analysis identified the top-performing drivers based on completed deliveries, revenue, completion rate, delivery efficiency, and customer ratings.
- Drivers with consistently high completion rates can be considered reliable operational performers.
- Drivers with high revenue contribution have a significant impact on overall company revenue.
- Drivers with lower completion rates or higher cancellation rates require further investigation.

## 💰 Revenue Insights

- Revenue analysis identified the drivers and routes contributing the highest completed-delivery revenue.
- High-revenue routes represent important business areas that should be monitored and maintained.
- Driver-level revenue contribution helps management understand how revenue is distributed across the workforce.
- Monthly revenue analysis helps identify drivers experiencing growth or decline in performance.

## 🏙️ City Insights

- Pickup-city analysis identifies locations with the highest delivery volume.
- High-volume cities may require additional drivers and vehicles during peak periods.
- Comparing drivers within the same city provides a fairer benchmark for performance evaluation.
- Cities with high cancellation rates should be investigated to identify operational problems.

## 🛣️ Route Insights

- The analysis identifies the routes with the highest number of completed deliveries.
- High-revenue routes can be prioritized for operational planning.
- Routes with longer average distances or delivery times may require route optimization.
- Comparing route performance can help identify opportunities to reduce delivery time and operating costs.

## ⭐ Customer Satisfaction Insights

- Customer review analysis identifies drivers receiving consistently high customer ratings.
- Five-star review analysis helps measure service quality.
- Drivers with lower average ratings may require training or additional performance monitoring.
- Customer feedback can be used to identify service-quality improvement opportunities.

## ⏱️ Delivery Efficiency Insights

- Delivery-time analysis identifies the fastest and slowest drivers.
- Large differences between a driver's fastest and slowest deliveries may indicate inconsistent operational performance.
- Average delivery time by city helps identify locations where delivery operations may be slower.
- Distance and delivery-time analysis can support route optimization.

## 📈 Performance Trend Insights

- Monthly revenue analysis using `LAG()` helps identify changes in driver performance over time.
- Drivers showing consecutive revenue growth can be identified for further analysis.
- Drivers experiencing declining revenue can be investigated to understand possible operational issues.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on these insights, management can:

1. Reward consistently high-performing drivers.
2. Provide training to drivers with low customer ratings.
3. Investigate drivers and cities with high cancellation rates.
4. Optimize routes with high delivery times or long distances.
5. Allocate additional resources to high-volume cities.
6. Focus on high-revenue routes to protect important revenue streams.
7. Monitor monthly driver performance and revenue trends.
8. Use customer feedback to continuously improve service quality.
